Draw a Bridge: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app "Draw a Bridge" (package com.drawabridge.game)

This policy explains what information the Draw a Bridge game handles. In short: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, a name, an email address or any other personal details, and it does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage analytics.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, unlocked paint jobs and bridge styles, achievements, daily reward streak) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Internet access and third parties

The app works completely offline and does not request internet access. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and no data is shared with or sold to third parties.

If a future version adds advertising (for example Google AdMob), this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe exactly what the advertising provider collects and how you can control it.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration permission to give short haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off in the game's settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
